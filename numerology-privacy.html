Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Information stored on your device

Moonveil Numerology stores the names and birth dates you enter for your local profiles and compatibility partners. It also stores your choices about whether a Y in a name is a vowel or a consonant, and which profile is selected. These details are kept in the app’s local storage on your device.

Calculations and data collection

Numerology calculations and reading selection run on your device. We do not operate a remote database, login service, advertising service or analytics service for this app. The app does not upload your names, birth dates or readings to us or to an online calculation or AI service. We do not sell or use this information for tracking.

Saved partners and multiple profiles

Each local profile can keep its own compatibility partner. A partner remains after the app is closed until you edit or remove that partner, delete the associated profile, or delete all local profiles. Selecting a different profile restores the partner saved for that profile.

Your controls and deletion

Use the profile icon to add, select or edit profiles. In the profile list, swipe a profile left to delete it; this also deletes the partner saved for that profile. In Compatibility, Remove second person deletes the partner saved for the selected profile. About & Help → Delete all local profiles removes all profiles, selected-profile information and saved compatibility partners from the app’s local storage.

Device backups

We do not provide account-based cloud sync. Your device’s operating system may include local app data in backups according to your Apple settings. Deleting data in the app does not remove older system-managed backups; manage those through Apple’s backup settings.

Other people’s information

Enter another person’s details only when you have appropriate permission to do so. An appropriate adult should manage information entered for a child. The app does not publish profiles or provide a public sharing service.

External links and support

Opening a source, privacy or support link takes you to an external website, where the website’s own privacy practices apply. If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and information you send so we can respond. We do not automatically attach your profiles or readings. Please avoid sending private birth details and redact screenshots unless the information is necessary to explain a problem.

Changes and contact

This policy covers the current features of Moonveil Numerology for iPhone. We will update it if the app’s data practices change. Contact Moonveil at moonveilassist@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
