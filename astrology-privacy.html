Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy describes Moonveil Astrology for iPhone.

Information on your device

Names, birth dates, birth times, selected birth places, coordinates, time zones, relationship selections and saved readings are stored in the app's local storage. No registration is required. We do not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service or remote database for this app, and we do not sell your information.

Place search

When you type or select a birth place, the place query or selected coordinates are sent to Apple Maps and geocoding services to obtain suggestions, coordinates and a time zone. Your name and birth date are not included in those requests by this app. Apple operates those services under its own privacy terms. Place search requires internet; after a place is confirmed, chart calculations run on your device.

Widgets

The app and its own widget share a local App Group container. The widget receives only the cache needed to display its daily reading. This is not a remote upload. A widget placed on your Home Screen may make that reading visible to someone viewing your device.

Retention and deletion

Keep individual saved readings only as long as you want: use their delete controls. Use About & Help → Delete all local data to remove the app's local data and widget cache. Deleting a birth profile does not automatically delete saved reading snapshots that contain its details; delete those entries separately or clear all local data. Removing the app can remove its data; system-managed device backups may retain a copy under your Apple backup settings.

Other people's details

If you enter someone else's information, do so with appropriate permission. Nothing in the app shares their profile publicly. Information about children should be entered and managed by an appropriate adult.
