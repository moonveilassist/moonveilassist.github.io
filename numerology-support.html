Effective date: October 17, 2026

Getting started

Enter your name using Latin A–Z letters and your birth date. Birth time and city are not needed. If the name contains Y, choose whether each Y is pronounced as a vowel or consonant. A different spelling can change name-based results.

Profiles and compatibility

Use the profile icon to add, switch or edit local profiles. Compatibility calculates both people’s numbers and has separate Love, Friendship, Family and Work views. The second person is saved separately for each selected profile and remains after you close the app. Use Remove second person to delete that profile’s saved partner.

Readings and daily numbers

Core calculations follow the methods documented in the app. Pinnacles use a supplementary method. Compatibility is an original number-based interpretation, not a compatibility formula supplied by the book. These readings are for personal reflection and entertainment, not predictions or professional advice. Daily, monthly and yearly numbers follow your device’s local calendar; themes can repeat as numbers cycle.

Personal Day widget

Add the small or medium Personal Day widget from the iPhone Home Screen widget gallery. It shows the daily number for the profile selected in the app. Open the app after switching profiles or changing time zones to refresh its local cache. iOS controls the exact refresh time. If the cache expires, the widget asks you to open the app rather than showing an old day’s reading. Tap the widget to open Today.

Appearance and accessibility

The app follows the iPhone’s light or dark appearance. If a keyboard does not appear, tap the name field again and check whether a hardware keyboard is connected. Report a persistent problem with the steps that trigger it.

Local data and deletion

Profiles and saved partners remain on this device; there is no developer-operated account or cross-device sync. Use the profile list or Compatibility to remove an individual record, or About & Help → Delete all local profiles to clear the app’s local records. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Contact support

Email moonveilassist@gmail.com. Include the app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. Please hide names, birth dates and other private details in screenshots unless they are necessary for the report.
