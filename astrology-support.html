Getting started

You may explore without a profile. To calculate a personal map, enter the date, local birth time and select a city from the place results. If the time is unknown, Ascendant and houses are omitted and noon positions are estimates. Compatibility lets you choose both people, select a relationship type, and save a snapshot. Reset restores the active user and clears the temporary second selection.

Widgets

Open the app to refresh the daily cache. Widget update timing is controlled by iOS; after a time-zone change or an expired cache, open the app again.

Your information

Data stays on this device and is not synchronized through a developer-operated account. Keep the app installed if you wish to retain local entries. About & Help includes the privacy policy and local-data deletion.

Contact support

Contact Moonveil at moonveilassist@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce it. Do not send birth details or other sensitive information unless necessary; redact screenshots first.
