About interpretations

Astronomical positions are calculated locally using tropical zodiac positions and Equal Houses. Interpretations and compatibility percentages are editorial reflection tools, not scientifically validated relationship predictions or professional advice. Daily text is generated from local editorial rules and sky calculations; it is not an external horoscope feed.
