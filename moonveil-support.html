Moonveil Tarot Support

Getting started

Choose Daily Card, Past · Present · Future, Yes / No or Guidance. Save a reading with its bookmark. Open the deck gallery to explore all 78 cards. Delete entries from Saved when you no longer need them.

Widgets

Draw today’s card inside the app first. Its widget shows that same card and orientation. The widget never draws a different card on its own. After midnight, draw again for the new day.

Your information

Data stays on this device and is not synchronized through a developer-operated account. Keep the app installed if you wish to retain local entries. About & Help includes the privacy policy and local-data deletion.

Contact support

Contact Moonveil Tarot at moonveilassist@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce it. Do not send birth details or other sensitive information unless necessary; redact screenshots first.
