Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy describes Moonveil for iPhone.

Information on your device

Drawn cards, card orientation, saved readings and preferences are stored in the app's local storage. No registration is required. We do not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service or remote database for this app, and we do not sell your information.

Network access

The app does not send your card draws or saved readings to a developer-operated server. Core tarot readings work offline.

Widgets

The app and its own widget share a local App Group container. The widget receives only the cache needed to display its daily reading. This is not a remote upload. A widget placed on your Home Screen may make that reading visible to someone viewing your device.

Retention and deletion

Keep individual saved readings only as long as you want: use their delete controls. Use About & Help → Delete all local data to remove the app's local data and widget cache. Removing the app can remove its data; system-managed device backups may retain a copy under your Apple backup settings.
